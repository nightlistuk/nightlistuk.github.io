Services

Overnight newborn care

Introductions to independent workers who offer overnight help with a newborn, so you can get some rest.

Bookings under 3 weeks: £250. Bookings of 3 weeks or more: £495. Charged after night one.

If a shorter booking is extended to 3 weeks or more, the balance is due.

Cleaning & housekeeping

Introductions to independent cleaners and housekeepers for regular or one-off help at home.

£195 introduction fee, charged only once you go ahead

Cooking & meal prep

Introductions to independent cooks who prepare family meals in your home.

How it works

For families

  • Tell us what help you need, anywhere in the UK.
  • We send you up to three people to choose from.
  • Workers set their own rate, and you pay them directly.
  • If we can't fill your request, you pay no fee.

Email us as a family

For workers

  • You pay nothing to be listed.
  • You set your own rate and keep 100% of it.
  • Families pay you directly.
