Family Terms

These are the plain-English terms for families who ask Nightlist for an introduction.

1. What we do

Nightlist is an introduction service. We introduce you to independent, self-employed workers. We are not their employer and not an agency of record. Any arrangement for the work is between you and the worker.

2. Our fees

  • Overnight newborn care, 3 weeks or more: £50 deposit, then £445 after night one (£495 total).
  • Overnight newborn care, under 3 weeks: £50 deposit, then £200 after night one (£250 total).
  • Cleaning: £20 deposit, then £40–55 after the first clean (£60–75 total).
  • Private chef: £20 deposit, then £50–80 after the event (£70–100 total).

We confirm the exact balance in writing before any introduction.

3. Deposit refund

If we can't fill your request, we refund your deposit in full.

4. When the balance is due

Before we make any introduction, you confirm by email that you agree to these terms. The balance is then due within 48 hours of the booking starting: after night one, after the first clean, or after the event. If the balance isn't paid, we may recover it as a debt, including through the small claims court.

5. You pay the worker directly

You agree the worker's rate and hours with them and pay them yourself. Workers keep 100% of their rate and pay Nightlist nothing.

6. Your own checks

We pass on what workers tell us but do not verify it. Please take up references and make your own checks before booking.

7. No replacement promise

We do not currently offer a replacement if a booking ends early, though you are welcome to ask us for a new introduction.

8. Your 14-day right to cancel

You can cancel within 14 days of paying your deposit for a full refund. If you ask us to start introducing within those 14 days and we make the introduction, you lose the right to cancel for the introduction fee. This is in line with the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

9. Your data

We only collect what we need to arrange introductions and never sell it. Please don't send us medical details, bank logins or passwords.

10. Contact

Email nightlistuk@gmail.com.
